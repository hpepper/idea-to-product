### Vocabulary

<!-- Bullet list of uncommon terms. Alphabetical. -->

- Acceptance criterion: Observable condition that shows the story is done, stated in the user's terms.
- Active story: Story in scope for planning and implementation.
- Architecture story: As an architect I need to <change> to <quality goal>.
- Retired story: Story no longer wanted; kept so its requirements can be retired or reparented.
- Story card: Captured idea with its root reason, before requirements exist.
- Technical debt story: As a developer I need to <change> to <quality goal>.
- User story: As a <role>, I want <goal> so that <purpose>.
- VoC: Voice of customer; the source quote or request behind the story.
